Spoiler Guard Lite

Configure server-side spoiler prevention and native client exemptions.

General

Turns the whole feature on. While it's off, nothing is ever hidden. Turn it on to let protection apply to shows, movies, and collections users opt into.

Image Protection

Applies server-side blur/replacement to poster and episode thumbnail images for unwatched items.
How protected cards look. Stock cards swap each unwatched image for a safe placeholder; blur applies a Gaussian blur to the original. Default: stock cards.
Gaussian blur strength applied when Image replacement mode is set to blur. 1 = barely blurred, 100 = heavily blurred. Default: 40.

Native Client & Scrubbing

Allows native clients (like Moonfin) to fetch unblurred scrubbing thumbnail tiles while preserving spoiler blur on episode stills.
